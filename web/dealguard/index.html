Understand the comparison

Total commitment

Annual recurring cost × term months ÷ 12 + one-time setup fee.

Annualized cost

Total commitment × 12 ÷ term months. This spreads the setup fee across the contract and allows a like-for-like comparison with the annual budget.

Requested difference

The supplier’s annualized cost minus the proposed annualized cost. The counteroffer is a request. A shorter commitment is not a cash saving, and supplier acceptance is unconfirmed.

Amounts are AED. Costs and approval policy are calculated by the server from the stored terms. Annual deal budget: AED 1,080,000 (BUDGET-001).

Your review workflow

  1. Start with an offer. Load the initial example or paste supplier terms, edit the message, then select Review offer.
  2. Inspect the decision. Compare the annualized cost and total commitment. Open the evidence and check required approvals.
  3. Review the exact wording. Approval is tied to the stored counteroffer. Replay produces a simulated receipt. Live delivery requires configured, authorized transport.
  4. Test the follow-up. Load the changed-term example and review again. Its 48-month commitment breaches the sample policy.

Changing mode starts a fresh negotiation. All business records in this workspace are synthetic, including in live analysis mode.
