## MentorOS ecosystem — primary demo

1. Open Ecosystem and point out the editable demo budget: AED 250 wellness allowance after protected essentials and savings.
2. Create a vacation or career mentor: describe the role, **Draft with AI**, edit and save. This is an actual custom configuration, not a fixed coach menu.
3. Choose Health and Finance, then **Try a gym & budget example**. Explain that distinct hosted agents first propose and then read/review each other. A real measured review took about 80 seconds; pre-run it or clearly label any accelerated recording.
4. Show the actual messages and Exa sources. Explain why a membership above the ceiling loses to a free routine or occasional visits; price snippets are not guaranteed quotes.
5. Listen to a generated reply. Approve the exact recommendation and show it in shared memory.
6. Use **Try sample receipt · synthetic**. Verify AED 8 + 12 + 6 = AED 26 and attach the analysis. No real purchase occurred.
7. Select the new vacation mentor alongside Health and Finance. Ask for a simple local picnic using those ingredients, one generated meal image, missing-ingredient search and an affordable local outing. Show the image tool output and peer suggestions.
8. End on the joint recommendation and approval boundary. Nothing was purchased or booked.

The standalone meal generator is available for a fast image demonstration; **Search ingredients with council** turns its description into a council request.
